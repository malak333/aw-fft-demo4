Battles

Combat in Final Fantasy Tactics unfolds on grid-based maps where positioning, timing, and job ability choices shape every encounter. Understanding how the charge timer and movement work is essential for newcomers.

Charge Timer and Speed

Every unit has a Charge Timer (CT) that rises in accordance with their Speed stat as clock ticks pass during combat. Speed determines how quickly CT accumulates, meaning faster units act more frequently than slower ones. When a unit's CT reaches 100, that unit gains a turn and may move, act, or do both. Movement and action are independently optional; a unit may perform either, both, or neither before ending its turn.

Charged Actions

Some abilities and spells require a separate charge period before they can be used. These charged actions use their own charge timing, independent of the unit's standard CT accumulation, and can resolve between unit turns once the charge is complete. This creates opportunities to disrupt opponents or set up powerful effects at unexpected moments.

Movement and Positioning

Each turn a unit moves according to its Move statistic, which governs horizontal movement range across the battlefield. Jump is a separate statistic that governs the elevation difference a unit can traverse, allowing it to reach elevated squares. Elevation also matters on maps with varied terrain; units at a higher level have a different tactical situation than those below. Facing influences combat, as rear attacks typically connect more easily. These positioning elements—Move for horizontal range, Jump for elevation, and facing—reward careful placement and job selection without relying on blanket terrain modifiers.

Jobs

The job system is the heart of Final Fantasy Tactics' progression. Players assign a Job Class to each character, determining the abilities they can use, the stats they gain, and the equipment they can wield. Characters switch jobs through the party and formation menus, which completely changes their capabilities.

Foundational Jobs

Two jobs are available from the start without any prerequisites: Squire and Chemist. The Squire is a versatile melee option with access to basic weaponry and the ability to switch to other jobs early. The Chemist provides healing and status-curing abilities, making a dedicated Chemist invaluable on tougher battles. Both serve as solid foundations for new players building their first army.

Advancement

More advanced jobs unlock through prerequisite job levels. Each job requires you to use other specific jobs at certain levels before it becomes available, creating a progression web that rewards experimentation. Once you meet the requirements, you can switch to the new job class through the party menus.

Job Points

As a character uses a job, they accumulate Job Points (JP). JP can be spent to permanently learn individual abilities and spells associated with that job. Learned abilities persist even if the character later changes to a different job class. This means thoughtful JP investment is key to building a versatile roster.

Ivalice

The story of Final Fantasy Tactics unfolds across Ivalice, a kingdom filled with political intrigue, religious conflict, and personal ambition. Ivalice has appeared in several other Square Enix titles, but each game presents its own distinct setting and narrative.

During the events of the original game, Ivalice is embroiled in a succession war between noble houses. The kingdom's fractured loyalties, the influence of secret societies, and the struggles of its people form the backdrop for a story about honor, betrayal, and the weight of destiny. The world is rendered in rich detail, from fortified cities to rural villages, each location contributing to the unfolding drama.

Characters

Final Fantasy Tactics features a large cast of characters bound together by war and ambition. Below are profiles of two central figures who anchor the narrative.

Ramza Beoulve

Ramza is the youngest of three sons of Balbanes Beoulve, following his elder brothers Dycedarg and Zalbag. House Beoulve is a noble family within Ivalice's ruling aristocracy. Intelligent and principled, Ramza is swept up in a web of conspiracy that forces him to confront the corruption festering within his kingdom's highest echelons. Throughout the story, he strives to protect those he cares about, embarking on a journey of reluctant leadership and unwavering moral conviction.

Delita Hyral

Delita Hyral rises from commoner origins to become one of the most consequential figures in Ivalice's history. Unlike the nobles around him, Delita had to fight for every opportunity, and his ambition is matched only by his resourcefulness. He forms a complex bond with Ramza that shapes both their destinies. Delita's story is one of survival, calculation, and the lengths a person will go to reshape the world.
